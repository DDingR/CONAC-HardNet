\begin{abstract}
  This proposal presents a neural-network framework for enforcing hard constraints in the presence of uncertain constraint functions.
  HardNet++ provides a systematic approach for constructing neural networks whose outputs satisfy prescribed hard constraints. 
  However, its direct application requires the functions defining the constraints to be known.
  To address constraints containing unknown functions, additional neural networks are introduced to estimate the unknown components and incorporate them into the constraint-enforcement architecture. The proposed approach will be investigated in both deep-learning and neuro-adaptive-control frameworks. 
  In the latter, the constrained neural-network architecture is integrated with online adaptation to account for system uncertainty while maintaining admissible control inputs. 
  Theoretical and numerical studies will be conducted to examine constraint satisfaction, approximation performance, and closed-loop behavior of the resulting framework.
\end{abstract}
